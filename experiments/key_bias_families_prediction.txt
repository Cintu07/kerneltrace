does the softmax-invariant key bias show up outside pythia? predictions, written before
scripts/key_bias_families.py was run.

the number is the largest per-head norm of the invariant part of the key bias, over all layers,
the same measure that gives pythia-160m 279. for scale, pythia's query bias stays under 9 and the
part of its key bias that rotary touches stays under 20.

what i know going in: opt and pythia were trained with fp16 mixed precision, bloom and cerebras-gpt
with bf16, and the training experiment says both grow it. i don't know gpt-2's training precision.

1. at least one non-pythia family has a model with an invariant part over 100.
2. opt: every size has an invariant part at least 5x its query bias.
3. bloom: every size has an invariant part at least 5x its query bias.
4. gpt-neox-20b: invariant part over 100, since it was trained with the same code and precision as pythia.
5. qwen2.5 has no invariant part by construction. its key bias, which the loss can see, is no
   bigger than 5x its query bias.
6. somewhere in the survey there is a model with a key bias whose invariant part is under 2x its query
   bias, so the growth is not universal.

added after the first survey pass (santacoder 384, gpt-2 small 9.5, opt-125m 7.6), before running
scripts/key_bias_other_models.py:

7. santacoder: bf16 perplexity at least 5% worse than fp32 on code.
8. santacoder: dropping the invariant key bias moves fp32 logits by at most 0.01, and brings bf16 within
   1% of fp32.
9. opt-125m: bf16 within 2% of fp32 with the bias in place. its invariant part is small.

added after 7-9 were run (santacoder: bf16 +0.1%, fp16 nan on all 32 windows, fp16 with the bias
dropped -0.0%) and after the survey showed qwen2.5's key bias sitting in the slow rotary dims, before
these runs. for qwen the edit zeroes the key bias in the dims that turn under 0.1 rad over 4096
positions. that is not exact, those dims still turn a little.

10. bloom-560m (invariant 183): fp16 or bf16 perplexity at least 2% worse than fp32, and dropping
    the bias brings both within 1%.
11. qwen2.5-0.5b: zeroing the slow-dim key bias moves fp32 logits by under 0.5 and fp32 perplexity
    by under 1%.
12. qwen2.5-0.5b: fp16 gives a non-finite window with the bias in place, and none with it zeroed.
