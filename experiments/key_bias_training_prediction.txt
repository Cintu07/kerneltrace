why does the invariant key bias grow? predictions, written before any of these runs.

setup (scripts/key_bias_training.py): a small pythia-shaped model trained from scratch.
gpt-neox architecture, rotary on 25% of each head, parallel residual, 4 layers, width 256,
4 heads of 64, so each head's key bias has 16 rotary dims and 48 invariant dims. pythia tokenizer,
wikitext-2 train, 16 x 256 tokens a step, 3000 steps, lr 1e-3 with warmup and cosine decay,
adam (0.9, 0.95), eps 1e-8, weight decay 0.01 on matrices only (biases and norms get none, like
gpt-neox), grad clip 1.0, eager attention, same seed and data order in every run.

runs
  fp32 adam            everything in fp32
  fp16 adam            autocast fp16 with a grad scaler, the way pythia was trained
  bf16 adam            autocast bf16
  fp16 adam eps 1e-4   as fp16 adam with a large adam epsilon
  fp16 sgd             autocast fp16, sgd with momentum 0.9, lr 0.1

the idea being tested: in exact math the gradient of the invariant part is zero, because it is
(sum over keys of the score gradient) times q, and that sum is zero. in low precision the score
gradient is cast down before the matmul, the row sums stop being zero, and the invariant part gets
a small gradient with a consistent sign. adam divides by the gradient's own size, so a small
consistent gradient still gets full-size steps. sgd does not, and a large eps does not.

"invariant norm" below is the largest per-layer norm of the invariant key bias at step 3000.
"rotary norm" is the same for the 16 rotary dims of the same tensor.

1. fp32 adam: invariant norm below 1.0.
2. fp16 adam: invariant norm more than 2x the rotary norm in at least one layer
   (pythia-160m was 4.4 against 1.4 by step 2000).
3. fp16 adam: invariant norm at least 5x the fp32 adam run's.
4. bf16 adam: invariant norm at least as large as fp16 adam's, since bf16 keeps 8 significant bits
   to fp16's 11 and the cast error in the row sums should be bigger.
5. fp16 adam eps 1e-4: invariant norm at most half of fp16 adam's.
6. fp16 sgd: invariant norm below 1.0.
7. gradient size, median over logged steps of |grad invariant| / |grad rotary|:
   below 1e-3 in fp32 adam, above 1e-2 in fp16 adam.
8. final validation loss of fp16 adam within 2% of fp32 adam. the growth does not hurt training
   at this scale.
