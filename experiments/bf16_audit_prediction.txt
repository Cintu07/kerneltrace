predictions for scripts/bf16_audit.py, written and committed before it was run.
48 ioi prompts per model, gpt-2 small, pythia-160m, pythia-410m, pythia-1b.

1. fp32: the batch-shape gap is under 1e-3 on every prompt on every model.
2. bf16: on gpt-2 the gap is 0.5 or more on at least a quarter of the prompts. on pythia
   it is bigger, because pythia's logits are bigger and the bf16 grid is coarser there.
3. bf16 with only the unembedding in fp32: the median gap is under 0.1 on every model.
4. ranking mlp layers by mean restoration with the sweep batched, the top 3 under bf16
   differ from fp32 on at least one of the four models. with the fp32 head they match
   fp32 on all four.

any of these can come out wrong. whatever happens goes in the readme.
